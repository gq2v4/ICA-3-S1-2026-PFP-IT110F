About Us

At Bubble Tea Bros, we're passionate about serving fresh, high-quality bubble tea made with premium ingredients. From classic milk teas to refreshing fruit blends, every drink is crafted to deliver great taste and a memorable experience. Whether you're stopping by for your daily favourite or trying something new, we're here to make every sip enjoyable.

Why Drink Bubble Tea?

  • There are many delicious flavours to choose from.
  • You can choose your own sugar and ice level.
  • Fruit teas are refreshing and contain fruit ingredients.
  • Bubble tea is a fun drink to enjoy with friends and family.
  • Tapioca pearls give the drink a unique chewy texture.
